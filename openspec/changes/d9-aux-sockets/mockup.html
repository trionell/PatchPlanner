OpenSpec change · d9-aux-sockets · defect D9 · revision 2 · for approval

An X32's aux in sockets are jacks, and they get a box of their own

On an X32 or M32 the Inputs graph draws the desk's local mic inputs and stops. The six rear aux in sockets are missing, so playback plugged into them, or a radio receiver on a line input, cannot be patched. This change adds them as a second panel of the desk: a box on the canvas, two fields on the phone and a box on the printed graph. It never touches the local count.

01 · today · read from the code

A source plugged into an X32's aux in has nowhere to land, because the desk's panel stops at Local in 32

The LOCAL box is sized by local_input_count alone. On FOH, an X32, that is 32 rows. The Playback pair and the host's radio receiver sit on the rear aux in sockets in real life. In the graph their only route to the Playback aux strip or to channel 24 is through a stagebox, which is not where they are plugged in.

Choose Today in §02 to see it: Playback and Host mic RX are hollow on both ends. The export has the same gap. It leaves every aux strip on the rear sockets because the graph cannot say anything else.

02 · the canvas · live

The aux sockets are a box of their own, because the canvas finds, selects and remembers boxes, not parts of one

Recommended: (b). A second pass-through box beside LOCAL, titled with the desk's name, badge LOCAL AUX, six rows numbered 1–6. It is built like LOCAL and a stagebox: one SplitPortRow per socket, the cable lands on the left as Aux in N, and the desk's own hop leaves on the right as Aux to desk N. The hop is drawn dashed, as LOCAL's is, and may reach any channel or aux strip. No footer, because LOCAL has none.

Until someone drags it, the AUX box sits 24 px under LOCAL with their left edges aligned. After that it keeps its own stored position, as LOCAL has since migration 043.

Try it: type aux in the search field under each option. In (b) the window moves to the AUX box. In (a) it moves to the LOCAL box, because a search lands on a box and not on a row, and the aux rows are about 600 px below that box's header.

03 · the two options weighed

Inside the local box the aux sockets would need a rule design/ does not have, and a search would still not reach them

Both options draw the same rows. They differ in what the canvas treats as a unit. Every action on the canvas works on a whole node: dragging, selecting into the inspector, search with the window following it, the stored position, the printed box. components.md §2 describes a node as header, ports, footer, with the ports at a fixed pitch. Option (b) fits that description as written. Option (a) needs an amendment.

The rule option (a) would add to design/components.md §2, under PortRow / SplitPortRow, with one token in graph/tokens.ts. It is not proposed: A node may divide its ports into named runs. A run after the first opens with a heading row one port pitch tall (18 px), with a border-t zinc-800 rule, its name in MICRO_LABEL at zinc-500 and its count at the right edge in 10 px mono zinc-600. A run's rows are numbered from 1. A node's footer counts its first run only. NODE_RUN in tokens.ts. This rule leaves the search, inspector and paper rows of the table unchanged, and each would need a decision of its own. Option (b) needs no rule, so (b) is recommended.
04 · the phone · drawn

On a phone the sheet's first routing field picks the panel, so the aux sockets are their own entry and never jacks 33 to 38

Today the phone offers no local jack at all. MobileChannelEditSheet's routing pair is Stagebox + In. A channel fed through a local jack opens with both fields blank, because resolveChannelRouting reads only stagebox and direct-source routes. So the aux sockets cannot be added “beside the local inputs” on the phone, because the local inputs are not on it.

Drawn: the pair keeps its shape. patterns.md §7d already allows two fields side by side when they are one idea, as a stagebox and its input number are. The first field is renamed Into and lists every panel a cable can land on. Stageboxes come first. Then a group under the desk's name holds two separate entries, FOH local in and FOH aux in. The second field, Jack, lists that panel's own jacks: In 1…, Local 1…32 or Aux 1…6. Saving writes the same two edges a stagebox route writes: source → socket, and socket → channel.

The list row needs no new component. Its subtitle is the chain's last hop, so it reads FOH aux in 3, the way a local route reads FOH local in 16. On the phone canvas the AUX box appears as it does on a desk. Tapping it opens its rows in the sheet, and patching stays a desk job (patterns.md §7g).

05 · paper · drawn

On paper the aux box prints where the canvas put it, under its own badge and with its own count

buildInputGraph gains a second FlowNode after the local one: title FOH aux, badge AUX, rows Aux in N / Aux to desk N, footer 6 aux sockets - 3 used. It is placed at the AUX box's stored position (patterns.md §8a: paper is a second rendering of one model). The local footer keeps counting local jacks only. Wires follow R1–R7 unchanged.

06 · models without sockets

A desk with no aux sockets draws no aux box and says nothing, as a desk with no local jacks already does

The count comes from the desk model and is never typed in. Where it is 0, nothing new is drawn on the canvas, the phone or paper, and no note is added. An absent panel is how the canvas already shows a desk with no local jacks (Slice 24: no jacks declared, no node). A sentence saying “this desk has no aux sockets” would be about the hardware, not about the patch. It would appear on every LV1 and Generic event, where nobody expects aux sockets.

07 · decisions · for the proposal

What approving this mockup commits the change to

1 · count
Each desk model declares its aux in socket count: 6 on X32, X32 Compact, Producer, Rack, M32, M32 Live, M32R and M32R Live; 0 on X32 Core, M32C, every LV1 and Generic. It is never typed.
2 · the box
On the Inputs canvas, a desk whose model has aux sockets shows a separate pass-through node: title the desk's name, badge LOCAL AUX, one split row per socket numbered 1–6, no footer, drawn with the existing NodeShell and SplitPortRow.
3 · labels
The cable side of socket N is Aux in N. The desk side is Aux to desk N. A chain hop through it reads <desk> aux in N, for example FOH aux in 3, on the sheet, the phone list and the inspector.
4 · edges
A cable may land on an aux socket from any source or pass-through that can reach a local jack. The socket's desk side may reach any channel or aux strip on the same desk. That hop is cableless and drawn dashed, as a local jack's is.
5 · position
The AUX node has its own stored position. Until it is first moved it sits 24 px below the LOCAL node, left edges aligned, or at LOCAL's default position when the desk has no local jacks. The canvas and the printed graph read the same stored position.
6 · find & select
The AUX node is selectable on its own and opens the inspector titled <desk> · aux sockets. Search matches it on the desk's name or the word “aux”, and the window follows a search to it.
7 · counts
Aux sockets are never counted in the desk's local jack count, its local usage or its over marking. The LOCAL node and its printed footer are unchanged.
8 · phone
The phone channel sheet's routing pair becomes Into + Jack. Into lists stageboxes, then for each desk with jacks a group named for the desk holding <desk> local in and <desk> aux in as separate entries. Jack lists the chosen panel's jacks: In N, Local N, Aux N. A channel already fed through either panel opens with both fields filled.
9 · paper
The audio PDF's input graph prints a node titled <desk> aux, badge AUX, rows Aux in N / Aux to desk N, footer 6 aux sockets - N used, whenever the desk's model has aux sockets.
10 · no sockets
A model with 0 aux sockets shows no AUX node, no Into entry and no printed box, and adds no note anywhere.
design/
No amendment is needed for (b). The node, the split row, the dashed hop, the grouped select and the side-by-side pair are all existing rules. Option (a) would need the one rule quoted in §03.
08 · scope

What this deliberately does not do

no USB sockets
Aux strips 7–8 get no jacks. USB playback is not modelled as a source panel.
no Outputs change
The Outputs graph's local outputs, and any aux out or monitor out on the rear panel, are untouched.
no typed count
Generic gets no aux socket field in the Rig drawer, and the console card states no aux count. A Generic desk that has aux sockets patches them as local jacks, as it does today.
no phone patching
The phone canvas still does not draw cables. The phone edits a channel's route through the sheet only.
no export UI
The X32 file writing a patched socket as the aux strip's source, or as user-in 161–166 for an ordinary channel, belongs to the proposal. Nothing in the export dialog changes.
no merged panel
The two panels are never one 38-row list on any surface, and aux sockets are never numbered after the locals.
09 · open questions

What the reviewer has to settle that a drawing cannot

(a) or (b)

Recommended: (b), a box of its own (§02, §03). Option (a) keeps one box per rear panel, at the cost of a new design/ rule and a search that stops 600 px short.

The phone gains local jacks too

Today the phone sheet cannot pick a local jack. Adding only aux sockets would leave the far more common local route unpickable next to it. Drawn: both panels, in one change. The alternative keeps D9 to aux only and adds no Into group on the phone. Assumed: both. That also means a channel fed through a local jack stops opening blank.

Aux sockets after a model switch

Switching FOH from X32 to X32 Core leaves cables on sockets the new model lacks. Assumed: the same B2 handling local jacks get. The cables are kept, the rows are drawn red with over against the aux count only, and the switch preview lists them as it lists local jacks.

Two meanings of “aux in” on one sheet

For Playback the sheet reads Strip: Aux in and Into: FOH aux in · Aux 1. The first is the strip pool, the second the socket. The desk itself uses both names. Assumed: keep both, because the desk prefix separates them. The alternative renames the pool option Aux strip.

The printed badge

Paper prints the local box as JACKS while the canvas says LOCAL. Drawn: LOCAL AUX on the canvas, AUX on paper. Assumed: JACKS is left alone here.
