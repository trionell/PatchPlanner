OpenSpec change · desk-routing · Slice 48 · revision 2 · for approval

A mix lives on the desk that mixes it

Today an event has one list of groups and DCAs, a built-in LR that every channel joins, and outputs that say what they feed but not what plays through them. This slice moves every mix onto its desk, checks it against the desk model's pools, lets a matrix pick its sources and an output name its mix, and gives each desk a recorder. It is one tab, worked one desk at a time, as tables or as a canvas.

Changes in revision 2
  • Redrawn on top of Slice 47. Revision 1 predated desk models. Every mix now sits in a pool of its desk's model, and a full pool refuses the next mix, naming the desk (§02, §03).
  • Six mix types: Main LR, Mono/Centre, Subgroup, Monitor, FX, Matrix. An X32 · M32 spends one pool of 16 buses on the middle three; an LV1 keeps a pool per type.
  • Routing is a tab, Desk Routing, with one table per destination type and a canvas of one desk. The Buses drawer is retired into it (§01).
  • Group → group and group → Main on LV1, bus → LR and → M/C on X32 (§04). Matrices pick their sources (§05). Outputs gain Fed by (§07).
  • New: the recorder, the card on an X32 · M32 and a laptop over SoundGrid on an LV1, fed by preamps and mixes and playing back into channels (§09).
  • The model switch gains mix lines (§10). The upgrade moves today's groups, DCAs and LR onto the desk, with nothing lost (§11).
01 · where it lives · drawn

Routing is a tab between Inputs and Outputs, because that is where the desk sits in the signal

A tenth event tab, Desk Routing, between Audio Inputs and Audio Outputs. It is a work panel (patterns.md §1): title, Sheet | Flow, the desk selector (B6), search, then + Mix. The desk selector uses Slice 47's key, so picking Monitor on Inputs opens Desk Routing on Monitor and back again. It has no No desk entry, because a mix cannot exist off a desk.

Not a drawer. A drawer holds what is declared once and referenced often (patterns.md §3). Mixes used to be that: a name and a colour. Now they are worked in: ticking thirty channels into sixteen mixes, then the matrices, the recorder. That is a work surface, and a canvas has to sit in a panel. Not a view inside Inputs. Matrices, the recorder and group-to-group routes have no channel row to live on, and who is in Amanda's IEM is read down a column, not hunted for across forty row editors.

The Buses drawer is retired, with its Rig-bar button on Inputs and its phone overflow row (“Groups & DCAs”). DCAs and mute groups are tables here. Inputs keeps a read-and-edit cell for a channel's own routes (§08).

02 · the tables · live

A mix is a column and a source is a row, so who is in Amanda's mix reads straight down

Every table has one destination type: Mains · Subgroups (Groups on LV1) · Monitors · FX · Matrices · DCAs · Mute groups · Recorder. Its columns are that type's mixes in slot order; its rows are what can feed them; a square is a route. The index row under the bar picks the table and is the pool readout: Monitors 16/16. One table at a time, remembered per viewer like the view.

A column edits like a row (patterns.md §2). Click its head: it takes the open tint, its editor opens above the grid (name, colour, width, slot), and its cells become the controls. Save writes the name and the routes together. One column at a time; the rest stay read. Rows a type cannot take are absent; cells the desk cannot route are hatched and inert (C3).

+ Mix asks the type first and takes the next free slot: an odd pair for a stereo bus or matrix on X32 · M32, any two consecutive slots on LV1. A full pool is refused inline, naming the desk, before anything is created. Try + Mix → Monitor on Monitor (all 16 Mon used), or a stereo bus on FOH.

03 · two engines · read from §02's state

An M32 spends one pool of sixteen buses three ways; an LV1 keeps a pool for each kind of mix

The pools are read from the desk model (Slice 47's profile), never typed. On X32 · M32 a bus becomes a subgroup, a monitor or an FX send by what it is called here; the desk itself does not care. So the Subgroups, Monitors and FX tables share one count, Buses 15/16, and the index shows it on all three. On LV1 each table has its own.

Stereo costs two slots on both engines, but places differently: X32 · M32 links odd–even (a stereo bus is Bus 5–6, never 6–7); LV1 takes any two consecutive Mon or Matrix slots. LV1 groups are always stereo and cost one. Generic has no pools: every type, any count, nothing checked.

04 · groups and mains · live in §02

A group feeds only the groups after it, so the routes form a triangle and a loop cannot be drawn

On LV1 the Groups table has a second run of rows under the channels: the groups themselves. Group 1 may feed groups 2 to 8, group 5 only 6 to 8. Every cell on or below the diagonal is hatched and has no control, so the feed-forward rule is the shape of the table rather than an error message. X32 · M32 has no bus → bus, so its Subgroups table has no such run.

“Main on/off” is a route in the Mains table, not a toggle somewhere else: the Mains table's rows are the channels and then every group (LV1) or every bus (X32 · M32), and its columns are L/R and C/Mono, or LR and M/C. A group with its L/R square empty is off Main. One construction answers does this reach the PA for a channel and for a mix.

05 · matrices · live in §02

A matrix picks its sources from mixes, and on an LV1 from channels too, twelve at most

The Matrices table's rows are the desk's mains and mixes. On X32 · M32 that is LR, M/C and the 16 buses, and no channel row exists, because the desk cannot send a channel to a matrix: Julkonsert's FOH puts Ambience on Bus 13–14 with LR off to get it into the recording matrix. On LV1 the rows are the mains, groups and Mon mixes, then channels, folded to the ones already used with Show all 36 channels. Each LV1 matrix column counts its sources, 5/12, and refuses the thirteenth inline.

06 · the canvas · live

Flow on this tab draws the inside of one desk, because no route here ever crosses to another desk

Columns left to right in signal order: channels (the source rail), groups or buses, mains, matrices, outputs, and the recorder. LV1 groups are laid out by depth: a group fed by another sits one step right of it, so feed-forward reads left to right with no edge turning back. X32 · M32 has one column of buses, typed by section.

Mix-to-mix and mix-to-output edges are always drawn; there are few. Channel edges are drawn only for the selection: thirty channels into sixteen mixes is five hundred lines, which is a texture, not a picture. Unselected, each mix shows its member count. Select a channel to see everywhere it goes; select a mix to see what feeds it and where it plays. An edge takes the colour of what it carries (P6). An internal route is a 1.25 px line, never a cable's 2 px, because nothing physical is there; the SoundGrid hop to an LV1 recorder is 6 3 dashed, as a network hop is.

Routes are not drawn by dragging. A route is a square in the Sheet, or in the rail: the rail shows the selected mix's sources and opens it in the Sheet with one action. This canvas shows one desk; the audio tabs' Flow still shows every desk (C1).

07 · fed by · live

An output keeps what it is and gains what plays through it, and the two are separate questions

Type stays the destination: what the speaker or IEM is (foh, monitor, sub, iem …). Fed by is new: which mix on the output's own desk plays through it. They differ often enough to need both: Julkonsert's Center is a matrix-type output fed by Mtx 1, its Sub a sub fed by Bus 15.

Fed by is set in the Outputs row editor and nowhere else; the routing tab and the canvas show it. The select lists the desk's mixes by type: on X32 · M32 Main LR, M/C, the 16 buses and the 6 matrices; on LV1 the mains, groups, Mon mixes, matrices and channel direct outs; FX are not offered on either. Several outputs may play one mix. A stereo mix on a mono output asks which side; a mono mix on a stereo output plays on both. An output fed by nothing shows — and is not a warning (patterns.md §5, Empty, expected).

08 · the channel's side · read from §02's state

A channel row still shows where it goes, and edits the routes a channel owns

Inputs' Buses column becomes Routes: the mains and groups it is on, then its DCAs, as chips in the mix colour, then a count for the rest (+6 mon · 1 fx). The row editor keeps BusMultiSelect, now listing the channel's own desk only: Routes to (mains and groups), DCAs, Mute groups. Monitor and FX membership is read there as a sentence with a hand-off, Edit in Desk Routing →, carrying the table in the URL (patterns.md §2). A channel's monitor sends are sixteen ticks; they belong to the column that reads them.

Moving a channel to another desk (Slice 47, B7) drops its routes on the old desk: they belonged to that desk's mixes. The editor says so before Save: Kick leaves 4 routes on FOH.

09 · the recorder · live

A recorder is the desk's own port: the card on an M32, a laptop on SoundGrid on an LV1

The user: “I select which preamps or outputs (groups, LR etc) should be routed to the USB card. Then I can also use the card as a source for inputs.” On both engines the recorder is the same thing: N tracks out of the desk and N back in. So it is modelled once, as a property of the desk: one recorder per desk, declared on the desk's card in Rig, routed in this tab's Recorder table.

Why not a Rig item. A Rig item is a box on the Flow canvas with cables someone draws, metered on its own card. The card is inside the desk; the laptop's only link is the SoundGrid network the desk already sits on. Both are routed by the desk's own routing page and written by the desk's own file (/config/routing/CARD and user out on X32; device-to-device routes in the LV1 session). A second desk never shares it. On the Equipment tab the laptop is a manual line, as today.

X32 · M32: the card, 32 tracks each way, read from the model. LV1: a recorder over SoundGrid with a typed track count, default 64. Generic: typed. Each track records a preamp (a port, named by the channel it feeds: Scen A 5 · OH L) or a mix (Main LR · L), and plays back into a channel, either for soundcheck (it replaces the preamp when virtual soundcheck is on) or as its source (walk-in music). Record every preamp fills tracks 1… with the desk's patched preamps in channel order, each playing back into its own channel. Past the last track is refused, like a full pool.

10 · the model switch · live

A model switch now says what happens to every mix, and still deletes none of them

Slice 47's preview Dialog (B1) gains the mix pools in its figures and mix lines in its list, in the same four words. Moved: a mix whose slot changes (LV1 groups become buses 1… in order, a stereo Mon on 2–3 becomes Bus 3–4). Changed: what the new desk does differently (a mono subgroup becomes a stereo LV1 group; C/Mono becomes M/C). Left out: mixes past the pool, and routes the new desk cannot make (group → group on X32, a channel into a matrix, a direct-out feed). Left-out mixes stay on the sheet flagged over (B2) and come back on a desk that holds them; left-out routes are kept and drawn as dashed red squares, used by nothing.

The mapping is the switch's: types in order (subgroups, monitors, FX), each keeping its slot where it is legal and free, else the next legal one, else past the end. Best effort, one function previews and applies (Slice 47 D4).

11 · the upgrade · real data, read from a copy

An upgraded event keeps every group, DCA and route, on the desk it was already played on

One desk (every real event): the built-in LR becomes that desk's Main LR; every other group becomes a Subgroup in id order, routed to Main, since today's model cannot say otherwise; DCAs move across; every membership is kept as a route. No mute groups exist, so none are made. Every desk is Generic after Slice 47, so nothing is checked until someone picks a model, and then §10's preview says what moves.

Two or more desks: Main LR on every desk. A group or DCA is copied onto each desk that has one of its channels, keeping name and colour; a group with no members goes to the first desk in Rig order. Each copy keeps only its own desk's members. Nothing is merged and nothing is dropped. No desk: the groups wait and land on the first desk added. Fed by is filled only where an output's name is exactly a mix's name on its desk (LR → Main LR); every other output starts at —, because guessing from the output type is what the export's revision 1 did and the user replaced.

12 · the phone · read from §02's state

On a phone a mix is a row, and its members are a run of 44 px ticks

A grid of sixteen 68 px columns does not fit 375 px, and P9 says the phone gets the same facts, composed for one hand. So the phone lists the selected table's mixes as rows (slot, name, members, where it plays), under the desk picker as the panel's title (B6) and a table picker in the bar's second row. Tap a mix: a bottom sheet with its fields and every possible source as a 44 px tick row. The same column, turned on its side.

The canvas renders, pans and selects; routes are ticked in the sheet, so nothing is withheld but dragging, which this canvas does not offer at any width. Viewers get the same rows and sheets with nothing to tick.

13 · read-only and empty · drawn from the same functions

A viewer reads every route and meets no control, and an empty table says what would go in it

Viewer: no + Mix, no Record every preamp, column heads are labels, squares are marks (P7). Contributors and owners edit, as on the patch sheets. Empty: a table with no mixes names what it would hold and offers its add (No FX sends yet. + FX); a pool the model does not have says so, not an empty table (LV1 16 has no groups.); an event with no desk refuses as Slice 47 does: Routing is planned per desk, and this event has none yet. 14 · amendments to design/ · proposed, not written

Five rules this slice needs that design/ does not yet state

None is written into design/ by this mockup. If approved, each goes in with the code that first uses it, in the same commit (CLAUDE.md). A1–A3 and B1–B4, B6, B7 are approved and used as they stand.

C1 · patterns.md §1
amends B6
A canvas of one container's inside is that container's. B6 says the canvas is not filtered and shows every container. That stays true of a canvas whose edges cross containers (the audio tabs' Flow). A canvas that draws only what is inside one container (Desk Routing's Flow) shows the selected container, follows the selector, and names it in its first column's heading.
C2 · patterns.md §1
the work panel
A panel of several tables shows one at a time, from an index row. One row under the bar, one entry per table naming it and its fill (Monitors 16/16), the current one underlined in amber like a page tab, no trough: it says what you are looking at, as ui/Tabs does. Remembered per viewer per event, like the view. On a phone it is a picker in the bar's second row. The strip states the verdict; the index carries the counts.
C3 · patterns.md §2
sheet and editor
A grid edits one column at a time, and a cell it cannot hold is not a control. Where a sheet's columns are containers and its cells are memberships, clicking a column head opens that column as a row opens: open tint, a GearForm above the grid spanning it, its cells become the controls, Save writes both. One column at a time; one row or column per panel. A membership is a 10 px square in the container's colour, never a circle (P5). A cell the container can never hold (feed-forward, a channel into an X32 matrix) is hatched in the row rule and has no control: it is a property of the desk, not of the user's role, so it is neither absent (P7) nor disabled.
C4 · patterns.md §6, §7e
+ components.md §5
Ten event tabs. Desk Routing between Audio Inputs and Audio Outputs, at both widths. §6's tab strip and §7e's section switcher say nine; both become ten. The phone's overflow loses its “Groups & DCAs” row.
C5 · components.md
§ui/Drawer, §ui/GearList
The Buses drawer is deleted, with BusesDrawer and BusSection (the memory rule: a zero-caller component is deleted, and the docs that name it fixed). §ui/Drawer's “narrow = Buses” and GearList's two-drawer history are rewritten; narrow keeps its other callers. The console card gains a Recorder Spec (Card · 26/32), which B3 already covers.
not proposed
The full-pool refusal is §5's Error row and Slice 47's hard-block wording. Over mixes after a switch are B2. The switch's mix lines are B1. Fed by is a Select in a GearForm. The edge weights reuse the canvas rule that dash is the medium. None needs a rule of its own.
15 · scope

What this deliberately does not do

no levels
No send levels, pans, pre/post taps, fader positions or mute states. A route exists or it does not. The export writes sends on at 0 dB.
no FX racks
An FX send is a mix with members; which effect it feeds, and the FX returns, are not modelled (Slice 47 kept FX returns out). FX are never an output's Fed by.
no cross-desk mixes
A mix belongs to one desk; a channel on FOH can never be in Monitor's mix. Desks share digital stageboxes (Slice 47) and analog splits stay processing devices, as today.
no drag routing
The canvas selects and reads; it never draws a route by dragging, at any width. Routes are squares.
no physical socket allocation
Which jack an output leaves on stays the Outputs canvas's cable. The export chooses X32 blocks or user out from it; this slice records only the mix.
no recorder transport
No arming, takes, file names or the DAW session. The recorder is a routing endpoint. On X32 · M32 only the 32-channel card is modelled; X-Dante, X-MADI and other cards are the same 32 tracks under another name.
no X32 direct outs
User out could take a channel direct out on X32 · M32; only LV1 offers it as Fed by, as the user stated.
no automatic routes
A new channel joins nothing. Today it joins the built-in LR; after this slice a planner routes it, because on a desk with subgroups LR is the wrong default for most channels (the wedding's own data has only Host in LR).
no export
Slice 49 reads this. Nothing here writes a file or warns about what a file cannot hold.
16 · open questions

What the reviewer has to settle that a drawing cannot

A new channel joins nothing

Today every new channel joins LR. Drawn: it joins nothing (§15). The alternative keeps “new channels go to Main” per desk. Assumed: nothing, because subgroups make LR the wrong default.

X32 virtual soundcheck

LV1 has a B input per channel, so playback is per track. X32 · M32 switches input blocks of 8 to the card, or user in per slot. Drawn as per track on both, which assumes the export writes user in. Unverified on the desk.

The LV1 recorder's track count

Drawn as typed, default 64. The SoundGrid driver's real maximum is unverified (UV) and may depend on the driver setting, not the licence.

Mute groups on buses

Drawn as channels only. X32 · M32 mute groups can also hold buses and mains, and LV1's possibly groups. Assumed: channels only in v1.

Generic group → group

Drawn with LV1's rule, feed-forward to higher groups, because it is the one rule that can never make a loop. Generic otherwise checks nothing.

The tab's name

Drawn as Desk Routing, because Signal Flow already names the derived chain view. Mixes is shorter but leaves out the recorder, DCAs and mute groups.

Unverified values

LV1 FX auxes as mono-or-stereo costing two when stereo; X32 Core's card; whether an LV1 group can send to Mon or FX (drawn: no). None appears in UI copy.
