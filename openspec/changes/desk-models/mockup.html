OpenSpec change · desk-models · Slice 47 · revision 3 · for approval

A desk is a model before it is a patch

An M32 and an LV1 differ in channel count, local I/O, mix pools and how they make a stereo channel. Today a console in PatchPlanner is a name, a role and four counts someone typed. This slice gives every console a desk model from a fixed list. The model fills in the counts, decides how stereo channels are numbered, and the patch is checked against it. Changing the model later is previewed first, and nothing is lost silently.

Changes in revision 3
  • One desk at a time. The Inputs and Outputs sheets list one desk's rows, picked in a desk selector after the view toggle; on a phone the panel's title is the picker. The selection is remembered per viewer, per event, and shared by Inputs and Outputs. The Flow canvas still shows every desk together. §04a, live in §04, phones in §08, rule B6 in §10.
  • + Channel adds to the selected desk, without a question, on any number of desks. Revision 2's add editor that asked for the desk, and its amendment B5, are dropped. So is the Desk column on Inputs.
  • Rows on no desk have their own entry in the selector, No desk · 2, and every desk's strip names them. Moving a row to another desk is the row editor's Desk field; the row leaves the list and a line says where it went (B7).
  • Aux is its own section of an X32 · M32 desk's list, with its own heading, count and + Aux. + Channel never lands on aux.
  • Decided: a deskless row needs a desk to save; a switch never fills aux and its overflow is a plain warning with the over flag. §12 keeps one aux question: what X32 → LV1 does with aux rows.
Changes in revision 2
  • A channel is always on a named desk (§04a; how it is asked changed in revision 3). Amendment B4.
  • Aux inputs are in scope. An X32 · M32 desk has two pools, Ch 1–32 and Aux 1–8, counted and blocked separately; the Ch cell reads Aux 3 or Aux 1–2; the editor's Strip field picks the pool. LV1 and Generic have none.
  • Decided in review of revision 1: the overall design, B1–B3, the hard block, and LV1 Classic as two models.
01 · the Rig drawer · live

The model is the first field on a desk, because every count after it is read from the model

The model is picked where a console is already declared: the Consoles section of the Rig drawer, in the card's GearForm. It is a native Select grouped by family (<optgroup>): X32, M32, LV1 software by licence, LV1 Classic, and Generic last. The drawer keeps its tier (patterns.md §3): size="sm" fields, 10 px mono specs, Button size="xs".

On a real model the four counts are no longer fields. They are read from the profile and shown as a read-only block under the select, and on the card as Specs. Nothing about an X32's 32 strips depends on its card fit or a licence, and LV1's licence is the model, so there is nothing left to type. P6's rule (a meaning is derived, never typed) applies to a count the same way it applies to a colour. Only Generic keeps the four fields, exactly as today.

Try it. Open Edit on a desk and change its model. A desk with channels opens the switch preview (§05) over the drawer; an empty desk switches at once. Pick LV1 80 to see the bus mode. Switch to the real event to see a migrated desk, and switch the role: a viewer has no Rig button at all (P7), so the drawer is not drawn for them.

02 · the profiles · drawn

Two engines and a licence ladder: every model on the list is one of them with different sockets

Ten X32 and M32 models share one engine and differ only in local I/O. The LV1 models differ by licence, which sets the strips and the mix pools, and the Classic adds built-in I/O. That is the whole list for v1. The table is the in-code data as it would ship; a value marked UV is unverified and appears in no UI copy.

X32 · M32 mixes
One pool of 16 mix buses, used as subgroups, monitor sends and FX sends alike. Each bus has its own assign to LR and to M/C. No bus feeds another bus. A matrix takes buses, LR and M/C, never a channel. Linking is odd–even only, on channels and on buses.
X32 · M32 aux in rev 2
A second pool of 8 strips, Aux 1–8, beside the 32 channels: /auxin/01 … /auxin/08/config in the scene, stereo-linked by /config/auxlink in odd–even pairs (1–2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–8). In the factory scene Aux 1–6 take the rear aux sockets and Aux 7–8 the USB player, L/R. Every X32 and M32 model has all eight, Core and C included, because they are the engine's and not the surface's. The sockets behind them are not counted (§11).
LV1 mixes
Groups are a separate pool from Mon auxes and FX auxes. Groups are always stereo; auxes and matrices are mono or stereo. A group can feed any higher-numbered group: Group 1 can feed 2 to the last, Group 7 can feed 8, the last group feeds none (checked in LV1 17.3 and confirmed by a saved session). Each group has its own Main toggle. A matrix takes up to 12 sources: channels directly, groups, Mon auxes and the mains. Channels send to FX auxes.
what this slice checks
Strips, aux inputs (X32 · M32), outputs, local inputs and local outputs, and the stereo rule. LV1 has no aux-in pool: every input is one of its 16 to 80 strips. FX returns are in the profile and not checked. The mix pools are stored with the profile and shown on the card, but checked by Slice 48, which is where per-desk mixes are created. Today's groups and DCAs belong to the event, not to a desk, so there is nothing yet to count against a desk's 16 buses.
outputs
On X32 · M32 the limit is the 16 patched output signals (OUT 1–16) that the local, AES50 and card sockets share, the figure the approved export dialog already uses. LV1 has no fixed output count: any mix can be patched to any SoundGrid device output, and those are stagebox outputs, which the stagebox cards already meter. The card shows by mixes and nothing warns.
03 · stereo and numbering · live from §01

A stereo channel is two strips on an M32 and one on an LV1, and the channel number says which

A stereo channel is one row with width = stereo. What it occupies on the desk is mixer_behavior, which today has no control and is stereo_channel on every row but one. Under a model it is no longer per row: X32 · M32 is linked_channels, LV1 is stereo_channel, written to every stereo row on the desk when the model is applied.

So the Ch cell follows the desk. On the M32 Live, OH L/R reads 5–6 and takes two strips; the next row is 7. On the LV1, the same row reads 6 with S in the width column, and the next row is 7. channelNumberLabel already draws 5–6 and has no caller; this slice gives it one. strips, not rows
Capacity counts strips. An X32 stereo row is 2, an LV1 stereo row is 1. Today's inputPatchStatus counts rows, which on an X32 would let 32 rows with four stereo channels pass as 32 of 32.
the next number

A stereo channel on X32 · M32 starts on the next free odd number. When the 32 channels are all taken it does not slide onto aux: it refuses (§04). rev 3 An aux row is only ever added with the aux section's own + Aux. suggestNextChannelNumber already knows linked rows take two numbers and also has no caller; it gains the desk's capacity, the pool and parity.
aux inputs rev 2
A row on X32 · M32 stores its pool beside its number: channel or aux. The Ch cell prints an aux row as Aux 3, and a stereo one as Aux 1–2, linked odd to even like a channel. Each pool is counted against its own capacity: 30/32 ch · 4/8 aux. rev 3 In the list the aux rows are their own section, under a heading with its own count, because they are a separate layer on the desk; §04 draws it. FOH's two aux rows below are a laptop on Aux 1–2 and break music on the USB player, Aux 7–8.
Generic
Generic keeps today's sheet exactly: the Ch cell shows the stored number, stereo rows keep whatever mixer_behavior they have, and rows are counted against the typed count as now.
04 · enforcement · live

A full desk refuses the next channel and names the desk that is full

Enforced means a hard block on adding and editing. No action can create a channel, an output or a local cable that the desk does not have. The server refuses it, and the client checks first so that the reason appears next to what was refused. What a model switch leaves over capacity is never blocked: it is kept and flagged (§05), because refusing the switch would force someone to delete their channels before they could see what the smaller desk can hold.

+ Channel is never disabled. A greyed button cannot say which desk is full, and on a phone it cannot say anything. When there is no strip left, pressing it adds nothing and puts the inline error block above the rows (patterns.md §5, Error). In the editor, the same check runs on the Number and Width fields, and Save refuses with the reason in the form. Where there is one obvious fix (a stereo channel on an even number) the error offers it as a button.

On X32 · M32 each pool is blocked on its own rev 2, and rev 3 each is its own section of the list: Channels 30/32, then Aux in 4/8, each under a heading with its count. + Channel in the bar adds a channel; the aux heading's own + Aux adds an aux input. When the channels are full, + Channel refuses and says so. It does not offer an aux input: putting a source on aux is a choice about where it sits on the surface, and the user wants it made on purpose.

Try it. rev 3 On Julkonsert the sheet opens on FOH, the first desk in Rig. Press + Channel twice: channels 31 and 32. The third time it refuses. Press + Aux: Aux 3. Open OH L/R and set the number to 6, or set Kick to 33, or open Pausmusik L/R and set its number to 4. Open Kick, set Desk to Monitor and save: it leaves this list, and a line says where it went. Pick No desk · 2 to put the two deskless rows on a desk. Switch Monitor to X32 Compact in §05 and come back: the rows past 32 are still here, in red.

04a · one desk at a time · rewritten in revision 3 · live in §04

A sheet is the desk you are working on, and the desk selector says which; only Flow shows every desk at once

rev 3 The user's ruling: separate desks get separate lists, picked with a selector, and the graph is the one place every desk is seen together. A desk is planned the way it is mixed: one surface at a time, numbered from its own strip 1. A list of FOH and Monitor together has two channel 1s in it, and every number in it needs its desk read beside it before it means anything.

The selector is a trough after the view toggle, built like the view toggle (patterns.md §1, the trough is what says these are alternatives for this panel). Each entry names the desk and its fill: FOH · M32 Live 30/32. It is in the sheet's bar only: in Flow it is absent, because Flow is every desk. The search, the strip and + Channel all act on the selected desk. With one desk the selector is absent and the sheet looks exactly as it does today. On a phone the panel's title is the selector, MobilePicker's title variant, because there the surface genuinely is one of N at a time.

This replaces revision 2's question. Revision 2 had + Channel open an editor that asked for the desk and argued against a desk chosen beforehand, because a choice made an hour ago would decide silently. A selector that is always on screen, in the bar above the rows, is not silent: the desk is named next to the button that adds to it, and the list under it is that desk's. So + Channel adds to the selected desk with no question, on any number of desks. Amendment B5 is dropped.

05 · the switch · live

A model switch is previewed first, because it renumbers channels someone has already labelled

Changing the model on a desk that has channels or outputs opens a Dialog. By components.md §ui/Dialog's test it interrupts: one question (switch this desk to that model, or not) that has to be answered before the form can go on, and nothing behind it is consulted while it is open. It is the max-w-md width ConfirmDialog uses, with the footer pinned (A1) below a body that scrolls.

The mapping is best effort and keeps order. Rows are taken in channel order. Each keeps its number when that is legal and free on the new desk. A stereo row on X32 · M32 takes the next free odd pair at or after its own number, and every row after it moves along as far as it has to. No row is placed ahead of a row that was above it, because the order of the strips is the layout of the desk. What does not fit before the last strip is left out, not deleted: it stays on the sheet with its number past the end, flagged, and comes back if the desk is switched to one that holds it.

Try it. The default case is the brief's: Monitor from LV1 64 to X32 Compact. Then switch it back to LV1 64 and read the preview again, or take FOH to X32 Compact to see local inputs lost, or take FOH to LV1 64 to see its aux rows move onto strips, or the real event's Generic desk to M32R.

06 · the catalog item · live in §01

The catalog item can suggest a model, and never sets one

07 · generic and the migration · drawn

Every console that exists today becomes Generic, and Generic is exactly today

The migration adds desk_model (default generic) and bus_mode to event_consoles. Every existing desk becomes Generic with the counts it has. Nothing is renumbered, nothing starts warning, and no notice announces the change: the Generic card states its own state on every visit, which is what a notice would have said once.

In the copy of the database there are four consoles. One is the real event's M23R with 32 / 16 / 16 / 8 typed, which are M32R's values exactly. Three are unnamed with all four counts at 0. All four become Generic, and the three unnamed ones go on drawing no meter and never warning.

A viewer reads the model off the status strip and never meets the switch

10 · amendments to design/ · proposed, drawn

Six rules this slice needs that design/ does not yet state: three approved, three for approval

None of these is written into design/ by this mockup. If approved, each goes in with the code, in the same commit (CLAUDE.md). A1–A3 from the export slice are assumed approved and are not repeated. B1–B3 were approved in the review of revision 1 and are unchanged. B4 is from revision 2, narrowed in revision 3. B5 is withdrawn. B6 and B7 are new in revision 3.

B1 · patterns.md §5 · A3 gains two words and a second caller approved

A3's state, a loss stated before an action, gets Moved (amber) and Fits again (no tint), and its second instance: the model switch. The box still takes the tint of its worst line; a box with only Fits again lines is untinted.

B2 · patterns.md §5 · a new state row: Over capacity, kept approved

A row past its container's capacity is not hidden and not deleted. It keeps its place in the order, its number turns red-400 and is followed by the word over in 10 px mono, and the panel's status strip takes the over-capacity tint. Today §5 has no row-level over state: over capacity exists only on the strip and the Meter.

B3 · components.md §ui/GearList · a derived count is a Spec, never a field approved

When a count can be read from something already declared (a model, a profile), the form shows it in a read-only block and the card shows it as a Spec. A count is a FieldStack width="count" only where nothing declares it. The Consoles section is the first instance; the same reasoning would apply to a stagebox model later.

B4 · patterns.md §5 · a new state row: Unassigned rev 2 · reworded rev 3

A row that must belong to a container and belongs to none is not hidden and not moved. Where a surface shows one container at a time (B6), the unassigned rows are the selector's last entry, named with the word and its count in amber-300 (No desk · 2), present only while there are any, and every container's status strip ends with the same count in amber, which selects it. 11 · scope

What this deliberately does not do

no mix checks
Bus, group, Mon, matrix and DCA pools are stored and shown, not checked. Slice 48 makes mixes per desk and checks them against these pools.
no FX returns
X32's 8 FX returns are in the profile and are not strips on the sheet; no row can sit on one. rev 2 Aux inputs, which revision 1 also left out, are in scope (§02, §03, §04).
no aux filling rev 2
Nothing moves a row onto an aux input but the planner: not + Channel, not a switch, not Put on. rev 3 A 40-channel patch switched onto an X32 is 8 left out with a warning; the planner moves rows to aux, or removes them, in the editor.
no aux sockets rev 2
The rear aux sockets and the USB player behind Aux 1–8 are not counted as local inputs and have no jack panel. The aux pool is a count of strips. An aux row's source is its Patch cell, as on any row.
no Generic aux count rev 2
Generic keeps exactly today's four typed counts. It checks nothing, so a fifth typed number would be a figure nothing reads, and Generic is defined as today.
no custom models
The list is in code. No user-defined profiles, no editing a profile, no overrides of a model's counts. A desk not on the list is Generic.
no card or expander maths
AES50, the card slot and P16 are not capacities here. Stageboxes on them are stageboxes, and their own cards meter them.
no undo of moves
A switch back does not restore earlier numbers. Left-out rows come back because they kept theirs.
no shared strips
Desks stay separate: each has its own channel rows. Two desks may take the same stagebox, as Julkonsert's do, and the box's card meters it once.
no name cutting
A name longer than the desk shows is listed under Changed and kept whole on the sheet. The export cuts it when it writes the file.
no all-desks sheet rev 3
Neither sheet has an All desks entry. Every desk at once is the Flow canvas's job, and Flow is unchanged by this slice.
no shared selection rev 3
The selected desk is per viewer and per device. It is never stored on the event, so it is not shared with colleagues and does not follow a person to another device.
