wave 6a · the bottom sheet · revision 1

The edge the thumb is on

Wave 6 is the phone, and it is one finding wearing twelve names. 6a is the first of its four phases and it builds no surface at all — two primitives, one hardening, six conversions. audit.md orders it first for a reason that is unusually concrete: “five call sites are waiting on it, and the correct implementation already exists inline in MobileNav.”

So most of this phase is a container swap, and the design work is deciding what the container is. Three things in here are not a swap, and they are what this document is asking about: the footer, where the written rule and the drawn specimen disagree; the label voice and the capability badges, which live inside the same six files; and env(safe-area-inset-bottom), which has been evaluating to 0 since Slice 21 and cannot be fixed from inside a component.

00 · the job

Two primitives, one hardening, six conversions

M8 is the whole of it: the phone's edit form is a centred dialog, and ui/Dialog is max-w-2xl, centred, with no Escape handler and no internal scroll. As the short confirmation box it was built for, that is survivable. As the phone's edit form it means a long sheet cannot be scrolled to its confirm button, and the confirm button sits where the keyboard opens.

Meanwhile the tree already contains a correct bottom sheet — anchored, rounded-t-xl, scrim, safe inset — written inline inside MobileNav's More menu. The pattern exists and is not a component, which is the condition A1 and A3 were both filed under.

Buildnew
ui/Sheet — the bottom sheet, and the phone's sibling of ui/Drawer. It does three jobs and decides nothing about any of them, which is the reason it is a component rather than a className on Dialog. §02, §03.
Anchor
ui/Drawer renders a sheet on a phone. Same props, two anchors, one branch — instead of eight call sites each discovering the question. Every drawer in the application gets its phone form from this one change, before 6c reaches any of them. §05.
Harden
ui/Dialog gains Escape and internal scroll. Both are strict additions, and both are things Drawer already does. This is the one piece of shared desktop code the wave touches, and it is handled the way Wave 2 handled ZoomControl. §06.
Convert
Six call sites — three phone edit sheets, the section switcher, the inventory category filter, and MobileNav's inline one. §08.
Fix outside
index.html's viewport meta. Half of M8's sentence — “the confirm button sits where the keyboard opens” — is not a component problem. §07.
Not in itout
No frame (MobilePanel is 6b), no status strip, no tab converted, no canvas, no !isMobile permission expression, no print branch. 6a changes what an overlay is; it does not change what is inside one — except where the same file has to be opened anyway. §08 is explicit about which those are.

ui/Sheet · the row-editor job, with every part it owns marked

1 scrim
The dialog's, not the drawer's — a sheet covers most of the screen. Tapping it closes. It also carries touch-none, so the page behind cannot be scrolled through it. fixed inset-0 z-50 flex items-end bg-zinc-950/80 touch-none
2 header
The drawer's header, at the phone's padding. text-lg title, optional text-xs subtitle, close on the right as a real 44 px target rather than a 14 px glyph. title is a ReactNode, because the inspector puts a MicroLabel and a Pill there instead of a string — see §03. flex flex-none items-center gap-2 border-b border-zinc-800 px-4 py-3.5
3 body
The one thing that scrolls. overscroll-contain so a flick at its end does not scroll the page behind it. No section inside it takes a height of its own — patterns.md §3, which Slice 32 wrote for the drawer and which is the same rule here. flex-1 overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain px-4 py-4 flex flex-col gap-3.5
4 delete
At the foot of the body, not in the footer. It scrolls with the content, so it is never under a thumb at rest. This is the decision §04 is about. Button variant="ghost" size="md" · text-red-400 · self-start
5 footer
Pinned, and it holds exactly one thing: the confirm, full width, labelled with the verb of the operation. When the job is a drawer this same slot holds the drawer's statement line instead — “Every row here is one line on the rental order · 12 items.” flex-none border-t border-zinc-800 px-4 py-3
6 inset
env(safe-area-inset-bottom), added to the footer's padding rather than absorbed by it. A confirm the home indicator sits on is a confirm that misses. Today this evaluates to 0; §07. pb-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom)+0.75rem)]
panel
rounded-t-xl with the bottom corners square, because it is anchored to an edge and therefore has two corners. border-zinc-700 — the Frame role, one step up from MobileNav's inline sheet, which used zinc-800. w-full max-h-[85svh] flex flex-col rounded-t-xl border-t border-zinc-700 bg-zinc-900 shadow-2xl

The whole API · six props, four of which Drawer already has

Height
max-h-[85svh], and no minimum. A three-row sheet is three rows tall. svh rather than vh because a phone's vh includes browser chrome that is not there — the sheet would be taller than the screen while the address bar is showing.
No handle
No grab handle, and no drag-to-dismiss. A handle is the platform's promise that the sheet can be dragged; drawing one over a sheet that cannot is a control that does not work. Two ways out — the close target and the scrim — and both do.
No cancel
The footer holds no Cancel. There are already three ways to leave without saving — the close target, the scrim, and the platform's own back gesture — and a fourth costs a third of the confirm's width. Dropping it is what makes full width literal rather than aspirational.
Where it lives
components/ui/Sheet.tsx, not components/mobile/ — ui/Drawer imports it, and a ui/ component reaching into mobile/ is the layering inversion components.md §5 exists to prevent. It is the same correction A1 made when MICRO_LABEL left graph/tokens.ts.
Also owns
role="dialog", aria-modal={scrim} — the un-scrimmed inspector is genuinely not modal — an accessible name from title, focus moved in on open and returned to the trigger on close. None of this exists on Dialog today, and building it once here is cheaper than five times later.
03 · three jobs

One shell, three tenants, three commit behaviours

On a desk, three separate things do these three jobs: the row editor of §2, the drawer of §3, and the inspector of §4. On a phone one sheet does all three, and the sheet is a container — containers do not have opinions about drafts. Each job keeps its own.

This is the test the component has to pass, so all three are drawn. If the shell needed a prop to tell them apart beyond scrim and flush, it would be doing someone else's job.

Job 3 · the inspector · no scrim, no footer, no draft — and therefore not modal

The scrim exception is a behaviour, not a colour

tokens.md §7 grants the canvas inspector one exception to the bg-zinc-950/80 scrim, and patterns.md §7f gives the reason: it is the one sheet read against what is behind it — you tapped the node to find out about the node, and dimming it answers the wrong question.

Implementing that honestly means more than setting the backdrop transparent. With no scrim there is no backdrop element at all, so the canvas stays live: panning still pans, and tapping another node re-points the sheet rather than closing it. That is the behaviour the exception implies, and it is why aria-modal follows scrim rather than being hard-coded true. The canvas is 6d's problem; the component has to be able to express it in 6a, or 6d re-opens this file.

04 · the footer

Where the rule and the drawing disagree

patterns.md §7d: “Confirm is full width at the bottom, delete kept apart from it (§2).” design/design-language.html §8 draws Save at flex: 1, then Cancel, then Delete, all in one row. Those are two different footers, and 6a is the phase that has to pick, because 6a is the phase that builds the thing.

At 375 px, three buttons and two gaps leave the confirm about 185 px of a 343 px row — and put a destructive control roughly 10 px from it, in the bottom-right corner, which is the single most-tapped area of a phone held in one hand. §2's delete kept apart from confirm is a rule written for a mouse; it matters more here, not less.

05 · the drawer

Same props, two anchors, one branch

ui/Drawer and ui/Sheet have the same shape: title, subtitle, body, footer, backdrop-and-Escape to close, nothing rendered when closed. The only prop Drawer has that Sheet cannot use is width, and a phone has one width.

So Drawer reads useIsMobile() and returns a Sheet. One branch, in one file, and every drawer in the application has a phone form the moment 6a lands — the Rig, the Buses, the Universes, Categories, the catalog's manage drawer — without 6c having to reach any of them. The alternative is eight call sites each discovering the same question, which is how NodeShell ended up with two copies.

06 · the dialog

Hardened, and left centred

ui/Dialog is the one piece of shared desktop code this wave touches, and it is touched only by addition — the way Wave 2 added onZoomIn to ZoomControl rather than forking it.

Escapeadd
The same effect Drawer has had since Slice 23. Three components now want it, so it becomes hooks/useEscapeToClose.ts — three callers is past the line Principle V draws, and the alternative is a fourth copy in Sheet.
Scrolladd
max-h-[85svh] flex flex-col, header flex-none, body overflow-y-auto — the sheet's own numbers, so a tall overlay behaves the same way whichever anchor it has. This is the wave's one visible desktop delta: a dialog taller than the viewport now scrolls instead of overflowing off both ends. Nothing in the tree is currently that tall, which is why it has never been reported.
Anchorkeep
A dialog stays centred at every width. The rule, stated once so it stops being decided per call site: an overlay with fields is a Sheet; an overlay that is a sentence and two buttons is a Dialog. M8 is not “Dialog is the wrong shape” — it is “Dialog is being used as an edit form”, and six conversions fix that at the call site rather than by making Dialog into something else.
Still open
Four Dialogs a phone can reach are not in 6a's six: ChainCell's hop detail, the two cable pickers, and EventMembersDialog. All four are short and read-only or near enough, and all four now scroll and take Escape. If any turns out to want a sheet, it wants it as part of its own surface's phase — not this one.
07 · outside the component

The safe area has never existed

M8's sentence has two halves. “A long sheet cannot be scrolled to its confirm button” is a component problem and §02 solves it. “The confirm button sits where the keyboard opens” is not, and neither is the inset the sheet is supposed to sit above.

frontend/index.html — today <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0" /> proposed <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, viewport-fit=cover, interactive-widget=resizes-content" />

One line · and two independent findings behind it

viewport-fit
Without viewport-fit=cover, every env(safe-area-inset-*) evaluates to 0 on iOS. There are two such call sites in the tree, both in MobileNav, both added in Slice 21, and both have therefore been inert on the device they were written for since the day they landed. Nothing about this is visible in a desktop browser at 375 px, which is why five waves of review did not catch it. This is A5's lesson once more: a value a sibling positions against has to be a value that exists.
interactive-widget
Makes the virtual keyboard resize the layout viewport rather than only the visual one, so a bottom-anchored footer lands above the keyboard instead of behind it. Chromium honours it; WebKit does not, so it is an improvement on Android and a no-op on iOS — which is why the sheet does not lean on it. The sheet's own defence is that its body scrolls and the focused field can be brought into view; the meta key just means Android does not need to.
Honestly
Neither line can be verified in this toolchain. There is no browser in it, let alone two mobile ones. This is Wave 1's stage-plot canvas again — the one thing in the phase worth a human glance on a real handset, and the spec should say so rather than claim a gate covers it.

08 · the conversions

Six call sites, and what rides along with each

audit.md Part 3 closes with the rule the M findings exist because nobody had: a slice that changes a surface changes its phone form in the same slice, or writes down that it did not and why. The same logic points inward here. Three of these six files hold M12 and one holds M7, and 6a is opening all of them. Rewriting a sheet's container and leaving its labels in a second voice means two passes over one file for one appearance — and the second pass is the one that never gets scheduled.

So 6a takes M12 and M7 with it, and the wave table in audit.md gains them. They were unassigned; that is the gap this notices, not a scope grab.

09 · what it costs

Twelve files, no endpoint, no migration

Backend
Nothing. Asserted by a gate rather than assumed: git diff --stat -- backend/ is empty for the whole phase, the way Slice 26 asserted the same claim.
Desktop
One visible delta, and it is in §06: a dialog taller than 85svh now scrolls. Everything else is asserted unchanged — Drawer's desktop return, and every Dialog shorter than the viewport, compared class-string by class-string through twMerge the way Wave 0 caught its own four rows.
Tests
There is nothing pure to test here — this is a shell. The gates are tsc -b, eslint, vitest green, the two diffs above, and a grep pair: <Dialog in components/mobile/ goes 4 → 0, and FieldLabel there goes 18 → 0.
Field test
One handset, two browsers, four things: the confirm clears the home indicator; the keyboard does not cover it; a long sheet scrolls to its end; and the back gesture closes the sheet rather than leaving the page. §07 says why none of these has a gate.

Deliberately not in 6a, each with its phase:

The frame6b
MobilePanel — the bar, the rows, the strip. The specimens above draw it dimmed behind every sheet because a sheet has to open over something; none of that is built in this phase, and the sheets land on the card stack that is there now.
The counts6c
design-language.html draws the section list with a derived count on each row — 16 ch · 3 gaps. Those come from lib/eventSummary.ts, which needs each tab's data, and the phone branches do not all load it yet. 6a ships the list; the counts arrive with the surfaces that derive them. Written down here so the deferral stays visible, which is the failure mode Part 3 names.
The editors6c
The three Mobile*EditSheets get the right container and the right label voice here. Whether they should be RowEditor outright — which is what §7d's table says the row-editor job holds — is a question about each surface, and each surface has a phase.
The canvas6d
The inspector job is drawn and speccable but not wired: no canvas mounts on a phone until 6d. What 6a owes 6d is the scrim={false} behaviour in §03 — built now so 6d does not re-open this file.
Permissions
!isMobile stays in three editable expressions M4 and two tabs still have no phone branch M9. Both are about surfaces. 6a is about overlays.
open

Three answers, and the spec writes itself

1 · The footer — A or B?

B is recommended (§04): full-width confirm alone in the footer, delete at the foot of the body, no Cancel. It follows patterns.md §7d as written and costs a redraw of design-language.html's sheet specimen in the same commit. A keeps the drawing and rewrites §7d instead.

2 · Do M7 and M12 ride along?

Recommended yes (§08). They live inside the six files 6a already opens, and neither is assigned to a phase today. The cost is about 60 lines and one type field; the cost of not doing it is opening the same six files twice for one appearance — and the second time is the visit that stops being scheduled.

3 · Is the viewport meta in this phase?

Recommended yes (§07). It is one line in index.html, it is the other half of M8's own sentence, and the sheet's safe-area padding is decorative without it. It is also the only change in the phase that cannot be verified here, so it wants to be in the phase whose field test is already a handset in a hand.

With those three answered the spec is short: one new component with six props, one branch in Drawer, two additions to Dialog, six conversions, a meta tag, and four design/ files updated in the same commit. No endpoint, no migration, no backend file, and one visible desktop delta stated in advance — which is the shape a phase that builds a primitive is supposed to have.
