The phone knows who can see this event. It cannot change it.

Overview already tells an owner standing in the room that 4 people have access and that you are the owner. Both of those are printed at 12 px in the panel's quiet last line, and there is nothing to press. The Invite button and the whole members dialog are rendered after EventDetail.tsx:85, which is where the phone returns — so on a handset neither element exists at all.

The reason there is no phone form is the shape. Manage access is a Dialog, and a Dialog stays centred at every width. A Drawer is a bottom Sheet below 768 px. This is the fault D4 found in the catalog browse, on the same rule, with the same consequence — so the fix is one component and not two, and getting the desk right is what hands the phone its surface.

Slice 39 states the test in your words and Slice 38 runs first, so this is the rule's first application: §02 sorts the slice's two overlays with it, and they land on opposite answers — the access surface accompanies, the removal confirm interrupts. Both are drawn here.

01 · the fault

The count is on the phone. The door is not.

Nothing is broken on the way to this surface, because there is no way to it. The nine MOBILE_SECTIONS hold no access surface, SettingsTab has no member UI, and OverviewTab fetches listMembers only to fill a sentence. Left: what a phone shows today. Right: what a desk shows, which is where every control lives.

02 · the shape

Nothing is waiting on you, so this is not a dialog

the test · your wording, 2026-07-31 · applied here first

A Dialog interrupts. One choice has to be made before what you were doing can continue, so it takes the centre, blocks the surface behind it, and leaves as soon as it is answered.

A Drawer accompanies. It is a companion workspace beside the thing you are working on: it opens over the surface without replacing it, and you go on working in it rather than answering it.

The old test — an overlay with fields is a Sheet; an overlay that is a sentence and two buttons is a Dialog — asked what an overlay contains. It misfiles this surface twice over: manage access has fields, and the cable pickers have a field while being interruptions. Whichever of Slice 38 and Slice 39 lands first writes the new wording into components.md §ui/Dialog; this one is first.

The arithmetic is stated because this project has shipped a value that was asserted and never multiplied out: tokens.md claimed px-3 py-2.5 and 44 px for a row, which is a 56 px row, and two implementations disagreed with it for five waves. So: 114 px per member on a phone, 68 px on a desk. Four members and the invite section come to roughly 700 px against a sheet's max-h-[85svh] — about 567 px on a 667 px handset — so the sheet scrolls, which is what a sheet does.

Nothing shrinks to avoid that. The email could be dropped to fit more people on a screen, and it is the one thing that distinguishes two people with the same name — so it stays, and the list scrolls instead.

What changes and what does not

The desk keeps h-8 and h-7. Amendment 1 is the phone's minimum, and amendment 2 is why: a surface on a phone keeps its tier's type and gives up its tier's geometry. Same MicroLabel, same 10 px mono spec line, same Pill — only the pressed things move, from 32 and 28 to 40 and 44.

The 44 px remove sits 8 px from a 40 px Select, which is close for a destructive control. §07 is what makes that acceptable: a mis-tap now costs a sentence, not a member.

09 · the edges

What this slice deliberately leaves alone

inviting by email
Still impossible, and now said out loud in the note instead of being inferred from an empty picker. Invitations to people who have never signed in are a membership model change and a backend slice; this one is entirely frontend.
transferring ownership
Not offered. The server refuses every write against an owner's row, so a control for it would be a control that cannot work. The owner's card carries a chip and nothing else.
leaving an event
Not here. Remove someone else and remove myself read identically in a member list and mean very different things; if leaving is wanted it belongs beside the event in the events list, not inside the drawer that manages other people.
who invited whom, and when
The database records invited_by. It is not drawn. A second line under every name buys a fact nobody asked for and costs the email its place — §7c's question, answered the same way it was for a channel.
searching the picker
The person picker stays a Select. It lists everyone who has ever signed in, which for this application is a handful of people; a search over a handful is §7b's search box that filters nothing.
the other seven window.confirms
Untouched. This slice converts the one destructive control it owns — and that one has no confirm at all today, so it is not even a conversion. D6 settles the rest as a set.
the desktop's densities
h-8 and h-7 stay on the desk. Amendment 1 is the phone's minimum and raising every control in the application is a different change with a different argument.
a phone drawer that does not edit
Slice 39 withholds editing on a phone because the canvas behind it cannot place a truss. Nothing is withheld here — the reason is the surface, not the width, and inviting someone from the loading bay is exactly the case that was reported missing.
10 · open

Three questions this mockup does not answer

open · for the reviewer to settle

All three are drawn as assumed, and none is settled. The header's Invite button is drawn deleted, with the Overview row as the only door at both widths; the exhausted sentence is drawn staying where the picker was, with only the durable rule behind the i; the two attachment rows are drawn without a heading.

Each alternative is kept visible beside its assumption below, because that is what a reviewer needs to rule on it. The spec is written from the rulings, not from the assumptions.

Q1 · Does the header's Invite button really go?

§05 deletes it and makes the Overview row the only door at both widths, which is the catalog's arrangement and gives viewers the read they cannot get today. The alternative is keeping it — renamed Access, shown to every role — so the drawer stays one click from any of the nine tabs, and accepting that one width has two doors. Assumed: deleted.

Q2 · Does the exhausted sentence stay in place, or go in the note?

§06 splits the two sentences: the state stands where the picker was, the rule goes to info. The brief said both go to info. If you want the literal reading, the Invite section renders its heading and nothing under it when there is nobody left, and the explanation is behind the i. Assumed: split.

Q3 · Do the two attachment rows get a heading?

On the phone, Readiness already sits over the five verdict rows as a MicroLabel and a rule. The catalog row below it has never had one, and the access row now joins it — two rows that are the event's attachments, unlabelled. tokens.md §7 wants a heading over a run of rows, and two is a run. Assumed: no heading, because adding one edits a shipped surface this slice did not come for.

approval

Approving this means: EventMembersDialog becomes a Drawer at narrow and acquires a phone form; components.md §ui/Dialog gains the interrupts / accompanies wording that Slice 39 will then apply rather than write; the Overview row becomes the one door at both widths and the header's Invite button is deleted; removing a member gets a ConfirmDialog; and the phone's role and remove controls become 40 and 44 px on a 114 px card. Approved 2026-07-31, as drawn — Q1–Q3 ruled as assumed.
